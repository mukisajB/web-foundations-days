About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas before they disappear. It is designed for speed, simplicity, and staying organized.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your browser.
  2. Type your note in the text area.
  3. Save or export your notes when finished.

Features

  • Fast note entry
  • Simple interface
  • Keyboard shortcuts
  • Export options
